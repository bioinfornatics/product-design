# End-to-end journey coverage

Read before planning multi-step product ideation. The unit of comparison is an interaction strategy from a shared trigger to the same useful outcome, not an attractive home screen. A public marketing landing is a separate scope.

## Required planning and review

1. Freeze a bounded scenario, responsibilities, shared corpus and success criterion. Choose a useful end-to-end slice; do not claim to explore the entire product from one scenario.
2. Map trigger → information/causes → choice/action → handoff/execution → outcome/evidence → return. Adapt these moments to the task; do not force seven screens. Every meaningful decision, handoff and result must be inspectable.
3. Define three different interaction strategies across that whole journey (for example guided progression, task-oriented workspace, or object/result dossier). Not merely different palettes, layouts of the same landing, or competing personas. Keep shared data, responsibility and outcome constant.
4. For multirole products, use complementary lanes on the same objects: who initiates, prepares, decides, executes and sees the result. Include material role handoffs. A home per role is not four alternative products. Record covered scenarios and explicitly deferred areas in a product coverage map.
5. Freeze ordered screen inventories including the final result and critical recovery branches. Record entry, data/scope, primary action/effect, next owner, next screen, preserved context/return and unknown/partial/blocked states. Preserve manual edits and permissions through conversational changes.
6. Generate all planned screens separately for all three journeys before completed comparison. Create one board per journey; label main-path steps and recovery branches. Add concise rationale, tradeoffs, assumptions and covered/deferred scenarios outside images.
7. Review continuity, business data, action/effect and outcome evidence. Unreadable or fabricated states need source revision, not a note declaring the source fixed.
8. Select a journey, reuse and correct its sources, obtain explicit complete source-set approval, then explicit G4 build handoff. Selected historical directions continue without forcing a new competition; missing comparison evidence remains disclosed.

## Coverage matrix and automation boundary

| Dimension | Contract | Verification |
|---|---|---|
| Scope | Multi-step default; lean only explicitly single-screen | Automated manifest/mode rejection |
| Main path | First screen reaches distinct outcome then End; no cycles/orphans | Automated ID/nextScreen traversal; semantic usefulness review |
| Recovery | Explicit criticalRecoveryScreenIds, nextScreen returns or exits | Automated references/cycle check; semantic adequacy review (empty list is not proof of no risk) |
| Sources | All planned screens in manifest order for three variants | Automated evidence coverage; filesystem hashes separately |
| Strategy | Alternatives change interaction across the journey | Semantic board review; declared behavioral evals |
| Responsibilities | Complementary lanes and material handoffs | Semantic scenario/product coverage review |
| Trust | Exact corpus, scope, action state, permissions, outcome evidence | Source-bound review declarations; not automated pixel truth or server authorization |
| Approval | Journey choice ≠ source-set approval ≠ G4 | Automated shape/binding; human identity and authorization outside validator |
| Capacity | Missing images means incomplete comparison | Deterministic missing coverage rejection; semantic honest-blocking eval |

Do not substitute a minimum image count for semantic coverage. Two screens can suffice for a short real task; ten home variants cannot prove a completed task. More than one material state, decision or handoff stays multi-step even on a single route or adaptive conversational workspace.

## Cost and resumption

Control cost through a bounded agreed scenario, per-screen bounded attempts, low quality where supported, isolated concurrent variants and sequential continuity, exact-path resumption and targeted revisions. Never auto-downgrade a product to three anchors. If tools, image capacity or time are insufficient, preserve plans and completed sources, name what is missing and keep comparison/build unapproved. Do not silently change provider/model or claim references were attached when only described.
